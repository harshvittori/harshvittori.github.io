The program

What is Claude Startups?

A quick explainer for anyone new to it.

In one line

Claude Startups is a free program run by Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI models, for young companies that build their products with Claude.

Approved startups get a free year of Claude Team (Claude for a whole team), API credits to run Claude inside their own apps, discounts from tools built on Claude, and direct time with Anthropic's AI engineers.

Anthropic expanded the program on 6 October 2026 to let many more founders in. It's open to bootstrapped teams too: no investor money needed.

Who can apply

  • A startup founded in the last 5 years, or funded in the last 2
  • A free Claude Console account (Anthropic's developer site)
  • A company email on your own website's domain (not Gmail)
  • A short description of what you're building
  • Following Anthropic's usage policies

Ground rules

How we play it

Never exaggerate

No made-up users, revenue, funding or partners. A false number is the surest way to be rejected, and can block us later.

No guarantee

Meeting the rules makes us eligible to apply. Anthropic reviews every application. Most answers come in minutes, the rest in 2–3 business days.

No deadline, but don't wait

Applications are open on a rolling basis, and Anthropic can change or end the program at any time. We apply as soon as phases 1–4 are done.

Keep secrets safe

The Claude API key never goes into website code or GitHub. It lives in a small server (step 2.3).
