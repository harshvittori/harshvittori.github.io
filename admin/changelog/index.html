Tue, 13 Oct 2026

7 changes · Haritt admin
  1. 1

    Admin now lives at haritt.com/admin

    Haritt admin

    The Haritt admin is now published on haritt.com/admin, the permanent address, next to the website. Sign-in needs haritt.com added in Firebase once. The old hvworld.haritt.com/admin keeps working for now.

  2. 2

    Page access for every admin page

    Haritt admin

    Team & access now lists every admin page (Brand assets, Covers, Design decisions, Infrastructure, Launch guide, haritt.com setup, Claude programs, Rules, Changelog), not just a few. Each of those pages now asks for sign-in and opens only for people the founder gave access to. Changing the social passphrase stays founder-only. The founder can also switch any of these pages to Public from Team & access, so it opens for anyone with the link without sign-in.

  3. 3

    Social Media Accounts

    Haritt admin

    New admin section for our social logins, grouped by brand (Haritt Group, Haritt Developers, HV World, or any brand you add). Each login keeps platform, username or email, password, where the verification code goes, recovery email and phone, and notes. Platforms and brands can be added from the page itself. Everything secret is encrypted in the browser with a team passphrase before it is saved; team members you give access to can show and copy it, and it locks itself after 10 idle minutes. Deleting needs the passphrase too, and the owner can change the passphrase (everything is re-encrypted at once)

  4. 4

    Admin on phones

    Haritt admin

    Fixed: on phones the top bar ran off the screen. Now it shows the logo, dark mode, your photo and Menu; Sign out sits at the bottom of the Menu

  5. 5

    Dark mode

    Haritt admin

    Dark mode for the whole admin: the moon button at the top right switches it on every page and remembers your choice; it also follows your device's dark setting until you pick

  6. 6

    Haritt branding for the admin

    Haritt admin

    The admin now carries the Haritt Group logo, Haritt red and Haritt Admin page titles and icon (same address); your photo and Sign out sit in the top bar, and the HV World logo on its website card shows in full again

  7. 7

    Haritt admin
