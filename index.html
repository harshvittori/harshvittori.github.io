HV Test

Who it's for

Anyone curious about how they handle real situations, and who wants a practical plan to grow.

How it works

  1. 1Pick a test. Start with the Maturity Assessment, and add your name, age and focus.
  2. 2Answer 28 to 30 everyday scenarios. There are no right answers: pick what you'd really do.
  3. 3Get your score, your strengths and growth areas, and two PDFs to keep.
Take a test

Maturity Assessment

The first test in HV Test: how you actually think, react and decide in real-life situations. About 10 minutes.

Real-life scenarios

A bank of 104 scenarios. Each attempt picks 28 to 30 of them, covering all 10 areas.

10 areas

Emotional control, accountability, self-awareness, handling conflict, relationships, decision-making, patience, empathy, responsibility and long-term thinking.

Score out of 100

Your overall score and a level: Developing, Emerging, Grounded or Highly Consistent.

Full report PDF

About 12 pages on each area: where you're strong and where to grow.

30-day Plan of Action PDF

About 10 pages of small daily steps. Download both PDFs together in one tap.

Fresh every time

Questions and options are shuffled, and a retake avoids the questions you saw last time.

Private by design

No login and no server. Your answers never leave your device.

Honest, not a quiz

Four believable options with real trade-offs and no "correct answer" hints. It's for growth, not a diagnosis.

More tests coming

HV Test is the home for every test. New ones appear on the same page.

How it's built

Real products, built end to end.

Design, product thinking, code and AI: every part of HV World was made by one person.

HV Vault

A React web app with Google sign-in. Each person's data is stored in their own private space (Firebase), with a local copy for speed. Also built as a Windows desktop app.

HV AI

Gemini, running through the site's own protected connection. It only proposes actions from a fixed list, checks each one, asks when something is unclear, and never invents a date.

HV Reset

A fast single-page app with time-aware sky themes and gentle sounds. It links to HV Vault through a safe inbox, so it never overwrites your records.

HV Test

Scoring and PDF reports are made right in the browser. No server and no login: answers never leave the device.

Designed for everyone

Works on phone and laptop, in light and dark mode, with readable fonts, keyboard support, and Hindi, English or Hinglish with HV AI.

Always up to date

All the sites, including this page, run on GitHub Pages and update the moment a change is published.

Privacy

Your data stays yours

HV Vault saves everything to your own private space, linked to your Google account, so only you can read it. HV Test never stores your answers at all: everything happens in your browser.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is HV World?

A family of three products built by Harsh Vittori: HV Vault for applications and opportunities, HV Reset for planning a focused day, and HV Test for self-assessment. Each one works on its own, and they connect where it helps.

Is it only for job seekers?

No. Students use it for internships, study days and self-awareness; professionals for deep work and staying ready for their next move; freelancers for tracking pitches and planning client work; mentors for giving the people they guide a clear structure.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser on phone or laptop. On a phone you can add any app to your home screen from the browser's share menu.

Do I need an account?

HV Vault uses Google sign-in so your data follows you across devices. HV Test needs no account at all.

Who made HV World?

Harsh Vittori designed and built all three products and this page, end to end. You can reach him on LinkedIn.

What languages does HV AI understand?

Hindi, English and Hinglish, typed or spoken. It replies in the language you use.

Can HV AI change things without asking?

No. Every change is shown first as a card you confirm, edit or cancel. Deletes always need an extra confirm, and you can undo the last change.

Are HV Test results a diagnosis?

No. HV Test is a self-assessment and personal growth tool, not a clinical or psychological diagnosis.
