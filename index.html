The problem

Where things break

Three things go wrong for almost everyone who is trying to grow: students, job seekers, professionals, freelancers. Each one quietly makes the next one worse.

Fixed by HV Test

02 · PLAN

Chapter 1 · Know yourself

HV Test

Self-assessment tests

The problemYou can't grow what you can't see. Most of us guess our strengths, and guess wrong under pressure.
The solutionShort tests built from everyday situations, with no "right answers" to game. The first is the Maturity Assessment.
  • 28 to 30 real scenarios, about 10 minutes
  • 10 areas, from patience to decision-making
  • A score out of 100 and a clear level
  • A full report PDF to keep
  • A 30-day plan PDF of small daily steps
  • Private: no login, answers stay on your device
All features
  • Maturity Assessment: how you think, react and decide in real life (the first HV Test)
  • A bank of 104 scenarios; each attempt picks 28 to 30 across all 10 areas
  • Areas: emotional control, accountability, self-awareness, handling conflict, relationships, decision-making, patience, empathy, responsibility, long-term thinking
  • Levels: Developing, Emerging, Grounded or Highly Consistent
  • Report (about 12 pages) and Plan of Action (about 10 pages), downloadable together
  • Questions and options shuffled; a retake avoids the questions you saw last time
  • Four believable options with real trade-offs, for growth, not a diagnosis
  • More tests will join on the same page

Chapter 2 · Plan your day

HV Reset

A calm daily plan

The problemBig plans fall apart at the first delay, and a lost morning turns into a lost day.
The solutionA day made of simple blocks, one task each, that bends instead of breaking.
  • One block, one task, with a big focus clock
  • Running late? Shift the rest of the day
  • Minimum version for hard days
  • Meals and breaks always built in
  • Linked to HV Vault: applications counted
  • HV AI builds and adjusts your plan
All features
  • Each block has one clear job, why it matters and what "done" looks like
  • Focus room with a calm countdown for the block you're in
  • Full-day timeline: done, now and next
  • Move today to tomorrow, or reset the day, without losing your plan
  • Work blocks stay at 90 minutes or less; lunch and dinner are never skipped
  • See follow-ups due and your week's review from HV Vault
  • Evening reflection: one good thing from today, and tomorrow's first step
  • Morning, afternoon, evening and night themes, with optional gentle sounds
  • HV AI keeps the exact times you give, like "2 se 3 outreach"

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is HV World?

Three products by Harsh Vittori that fix the three links where growth usually breaks: HV Test to know yourself, HV Reset to plan your day, and HV Vault to act on opportunities.

Do I have to use all three?

No. Each product works on its own. Use the one that fixes your weakest link, and add the others when you're ready.

Is it only for job seekers?

No. Students use it for internships and study days, professionals for deep work and their next move, freelancers for tracking pitches, and mentors to give the people they guide a clear structure.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything opens in a browser on phone or laptop. You can add any app to your phone's home screen from the browser menu.

Is my data private?

HV Vault stores your data in your own private space linked to your Google account. HV Test never stores your answers: everything happens on your device.

Who made HV World?
